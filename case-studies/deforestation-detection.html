01 / Overview

At a glance

An AI and data-science project focused on identifying signs of deforestation.

What this demonstrates: Illustrates the connection between data preprocessing, AI prediction and real-world environmental interpretation.

Case-study scope: factual project purpose is based on Novin's description. The workflow shown below is a proposed or general technical direction, not proof that every step is implemented.

02 / Context

The problem and the objective

The challenge

Forest-cover changes can be difficult to monitor consistently. Imagery contains shadows, clouds, seasonal changes and varying resolution that can confuse a prediction model.

The goal

Explore an AI-assisted way to flag potential forest change for further human review.

03 / Reasoning

05 / Judgment

Important design and research decisions

Avoid false certainty

Change detection is not always deforestation. Weather, seasonal variation and image quality matter.

Explain provenance

Any final claim must identify imagery source, timeframe, resolution and evaluation method.
